Our services

General descriptions only. Nothing here is a diagnosis or personal medical advice.

Psychiatric Consultation

A structured first meeting to understand your concerns, history and goals.

What it generally involves: A conversation about your concerns, background and what support may suit you.

  • Listening-first conversation about your concerns
  • Review of personal and medical history
  • Discussion of goals and what support may help
  • Clear explanation of possible next steps
  • A plan agreed together, not imposed
Request this consultation

Anxiety and Depression Care

Respectful assessment and ongoing support for persistent low mood or worry.

What it generally involves: A clinician-led discussion of symptoms and a care plan agreed together.

  • Respectful discussion of mood, worry and daily impact
  • Review of how long and how often symptoms occur
  • Consideration of lifestyle, sleep and stressors
  • Options explained in plain language
  • Regular reviews to see how you are doing
Request this consultation

Stress and Emotional Wellbeing

Space to talk through pressure, burnout or life changes.

What it generally involves: Exploring stressors and practical next steps with your psychiatrist.

  • Talking through work, family or life pressure
  • Spotting patterns that add to stress
  • Practical coping ideas to discuss with your clinician
  • Guidance on when further support may help
Request this consultation

Sleep-Related Concerns

Support when sleep difficulties affect daily life.

What it generally involves: Reviewing sleep patterns and related factors in a clinical setting.

  • Review of sleep routine and habits
  • Looking at stress, mood and health factors
  • Discussion of daytime tiredness and focus
  • Simple routine changes to talk through
  • Follow-up to review changes
Request this consultation

Follow-up Consultation

Continued review for existing patients, when appropriate.

What it generally involves: Reviewing progress and adjusting the plan with your clinician.

  • For existing patients of the clinic
  • Review of progress since the last visit
  • Open discussion of how the plan feels
  • Adjustments made together where appropriate
  • Next review date agreed
Request this consultation

Online Consultation

Remote consultations. (sample: confirm availability)

What it generally involves: A video or phone appointment, where the clinic offers it.

  • Consultation by video or phone, if offered
  • Quiet, private space recommended on your side
  • Same listening-first approach
  • Availability confirmed when booking
  • Useful when travel is difficult
